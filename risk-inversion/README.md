# Holding Risk & Inversion Department

This is the RemotePay Holdings control plane for risk, inversion, red-team, compliance, incident response, runtime authorization, agent evaluation, active memory governance, and AI governance.

## Operating rule

Every control follows:

EXISTS → WORKS → VERIFIED → LIVE

No control is represented as LIVE without runtime evidence.

## Departments

- Risk Director
- Inversion Analyst
- Red Team Lead
- Compliance Auditor
- Incident Commander

## Cross-platform controls

1. Runtime Identity & Intent Control
2. Agent Evaluation & Observability
3. Active Memory Reconstruction
4. Board-level AI Governance

Brand liaisons remain thin adapters in product repositories. The Holding department owns policy, escalation, evidence contracts, and reporting.

## Safety boundary

This repository does not grant legal status to an AI system or AI digital twin. Human directors/board members remain the legal decision-makers unless applicable law and corporate instruments establish otherwise.

References to King IV/V, the Companies Act, or other regulatory requirements are governance mappings pending professional legal review unless backed by current primary-source evidence.

## No fabricated proof

A risk record, control, audit, incident, evaluation or governance item is not proof merely because it exists in source code. Runtime/provider evidence is required for VERIFIED/LIVE.
